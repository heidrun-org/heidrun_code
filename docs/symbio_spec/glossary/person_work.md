## 1. Definitions

### 1.1. Work of one person tab

The tab of the Statistics window for viewing work on selected repositories or the estimated cost of coding agents.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-14).

### 1.2. Source of the Work of one person tab

The kind of data selected for the chart in Work of one person, either Git history or AI cost.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-18).

### 1.3. Git history

The source for the selected person’s commits, pull requests, issues, and lines added and deleted.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-22).

### 1.4. Metric of the Git history

The choice of what the Git history chart counts.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-26).

### 1.5. AI cost

The source for viewing the estimated cost or token usage of Chats by coding agent.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-30).

Review note: Review the difference between AI cost here and Estimated cost of a chat in Past chats.

### 1.6. Workspace selector of the Work of one person tab

The control for choosing the Workspaces included in the tab.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-34).

### 1.7. Unit of the AI cost

The choice between showing estimated US dollars and showing tokens.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-38).

### 1.8. Person

An author whose contributions are counted in Git history.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-42).

### 1.9. Person chooser

The control for selecting the Person shown by the Git history chart.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-46).

### 1.10. Time range of the Work of one person tab

The period selected for the Work of one person chart.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-50).

### 1.11. Day

The period from midnight to the next midnight in the computer’s time zone.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-54).

### 1.12. Slot length of the Work of one person tab

The selected duration of a Slot in the Work of one person chart.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-62).

Review note: Check the phrase both time ranges against the four available ranges.

### 1.13. Totals

The sums shown for the selected range and source in the Work of one person tab.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-66).

### 1.14. Bar chart of the work of one person

The chart that shows Git history or AI cost with one bar per Slot.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/person_work.md#source-line-74).

Related definitions: [Slot](statistics.md#13-slot), [Average per day](statistics.md#14-average-per-day).
