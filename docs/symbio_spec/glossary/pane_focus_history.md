## 1. Definitions

### 1.1. Top bar

The row of controls at the top of the Heidrun Code window.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/pane_focus_history.md#source-line-12).

### 1.2. Pane focus history

The ordered list of Panes that had focus, with one current entry.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/pane_focus_history.md#source-line-16).

### 1.3. Back button

The Top bar control for moving to the previous entry in Pane focus history.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/pane_focus_history.md#source-line-20).

### 1.4. Forward button

The Top bar control for moving to the next entry in Pane focus history.

[Transferred source](../proposals/glossary_migration_sources/pane_focus_history.md#source-line-24).
