## 1. Decision

Average per day is the total divided by the number of days in the selected time range, including days with no activity.

## 2. Reason

The definition works for the selected range. Existing tests exercise three- and fourteen-day ranges in addition to the seven- and thirty-day ranges discussed in the review.
