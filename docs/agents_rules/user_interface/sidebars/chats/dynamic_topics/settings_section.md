This file is a part of the rules of the user interface of Heidrun Code. The words are defined in [glossary/index.md](../../../../glossary/index.md) and [glossary/dynamic_topics.md](../../../../glossary/dynamic_topics.md).

- The Settings window has a section "Dynamic topics", the seventh section of its sidebar, just before the section "Mobile access" (icon `tags`). The first control of the section is the option "Enable dynamic topics": a Bootstrap 6 switch (`switch`) with the label "Enable dynamic topics", below the title of the section and above the title "Extraction method". The switch is the option `dynamicTopics` of the settings, and the default is on, so the behavior does not change for the developers who already use Heidrun Code. The switch has no title and no help paragraph. A circled question mark after its label opens an information card with three blocks, "What it is", "When the option is on", and "When the option is off", as [settings_information_cards.md](../../../window/settings_information_cards.md) says. While it is off, the other controls of the section do not change: they stay enabled and keep their values. The rules of what the option does are in [display.md](display.md). The section also holds the choice "Extraction method": a Bootstrap 6 button group of two radio inputs (`btn-group` with `btn-check btn-outline theme-secondary` labels), "Keyword" and "Language model". The choice is the option `dynamicTopicExtraction` of the settings, and the default is "keyword". Both radio inputs are enabled. The title of the button "Language model" says that the coding agent `codex` or `claude` that is installed on this computer finds the topics. The more menu has no entry for it.
- The title "Extraction method" is followed, on the same line, by a circled question mark (the icon `question-circle`, in muted color). The section has no help paragraph under the button group: the circled question mark holds the help. A click on it opens an information card below it, with a title (the question mark icon and the text "Extraction method"), then two blocks: "Keyword" says that the topics of each chat come from the words of its title, of the first messages that the developer typed, of the folders of the files that the coding agent wrote, and of the titles of its issues and pull requests, and that nothing leaves this computer; "Language model" says that a language model finds the topics, and that Heidrun Code sends the title and the first messages that the developer typed, of the chats that the list shows, and nothing else, to the coding agent `codex` or `claude` that is installed on this computer. The look of the blocks is in [sidebars_appearance.md](../../../../web_styling/sidebars_appearance.md).
- The information card of a circled question mark in the Settings window follows the rules of every information card: it opens on a click, and a second click on the circled question mark, the key Escape, a click outside, and the loss of the keyboard focus close it. It opens below the circled question mark, with the middle of the card and its arrow under the middle of the circled question mark, and it stays inside the window. The component `InformationCard` has the property `placement` for this: "start" (the default) opens the card at the left of its part, "bottom" opens it below with its left edge at the left edge of its part, and "bottom-center" opens it below with its middle under the middle of its part. Every circled question mark uses "bottom-center".
