# Past chats sidebar section: the dates line

- Created: 13 October 2026
- Last updated: 23 October 2026
- Status: Maintained rules for agents and tools
- Related framing: [Project instructions](../../../../../../AGENTS.md)
- Related rules: [Glossary of Heidrun Code](../../../../glossary/index.md)
- Related issue: [issue 442](https://github.com/heidrun-org/heidrun/issues/442)

This file is a part of the rules of the user interface of Heidrun Code. The words are defined in [glossary/index.md](../../../../glossary/index.md).

- The dates line has an information card, opened the same way as the information card of the files line: the whole line is the part that gets a background under the pointer, and a click on the line opens the information card.
- The information card of the dates line has a title with the clock icon and the text "Dates and messages", then one row for each fact, with the name of the fact in muted color at the left and its value at the right: "Started" and "Last activity" say how long ago the date was with one unit ("2 days ago", "Just now" under one minute), and each one ends with a small question mark (the HTML tag `small` around the icon `question-circle`) whose title is the full date with the day of the week and the time; "Duration" has one unit, rounded to the nearest ("12 minutes", "3 hours", "Less than a minute"); and "Messages from you" is the number of messages. Four rows about the tokens of the chat follow, each one with a number of tokens in a monospace font, so that the numbers stay aligned: "Input tokens" (every input token that was sent, with the cached ones), "Cached input tokens" (the input tokens that the coding agent read from its cache, left out when there are none), "Output tokens", and "Estimated cost" (an amount of money with the dollar sign, or "Less than $0.01" under one cent). A number of tokens under 1000 shows as it is. A bigger number is short, with two digits after the decimal point and a unit: "KT" for thousands, "MT" for millions, "BT" for billions, and "TT" for trillions ("20.61 KT", "3.10 MT", "1.23 BT"), and a number that rounds up to 1000 of a unit moves to the next unit. A short number has a small circled question mark after it (the same icon as the dates) whose title is the full number with the separators of the language, a space, and the word "tokens" ("3,103,109 tokens"). When the chat used one model that has no known price, the "Estimated cost" row is left out and the tokens show without a cost. When the cost shows, a muted note in small letters follows the rows of the facts and says on the screen that the cost is an estimate, that each model is paid by token at its public price, and that a fixed subscription pays something else. A fact that the chat does not have is left out. The text "ago" is made again each time the information card opens. A muted row "Your first messages" follows, then one row with a chat icon for each of the first three messages that the developer typed, in order, each one on one line of text cut to 300 characters by the program. The background of a row under the pointer is in [sidebars_appearance.md](../../../../web_styling/sidebars_appearance.md). The information card shows no folder and no file: the files line shows the files. The name of a fact stays on one line.
